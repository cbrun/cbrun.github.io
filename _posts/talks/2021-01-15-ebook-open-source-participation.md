This Eclipse Foundation eBook looks beyond simply consuming open-source software. It examines how active participation helps organizations develop skills, influence the technologies they depend on, and support healthier projects. My contribution draws on experience building a business alongside Eclipse projects.

For a broader framework connecting participation, governance, and durable business value, see [Industrial open source: governance, sustainability, and durable value]({{ site.url }}/open-source-industrial/).
