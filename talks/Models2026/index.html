01 / Engineering semantics

A model gives AI
engineering
semantics.

[Talk 1:50–2:20]

A model tells us that this function is allocated to this component. That a capability is realized through these scenarios. That components exchange information. That one abstraction level realizes another.

Those relationships have a precise meaning in engineering. They give an agent paths it can follow when answering a question about the system.

Three claims

Engineering semantics.

Model-enabled reasoning.

[Talk 17:30–17:45]

So, three claims. Models give agents engineering semantics. They support reasoning about a design. And methods guide how agents work.

The structure we put into models becomes accessible through natural language.

This structure, the tools, and the method associated are the common ground in between the system engineer and the agent. Models are a perfect match for efficient work with an LLM, bringing back determinism, validation on top of this automated generation.
